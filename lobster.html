The Original Lobster House is a premium seafood restaurant in Cozumel established in 1983. Known for its fresh Caribbean lobster, romantic tropical atmosphere, candlelight dining, live music, and upscale island vibe. The restaurant blends fine seafood dining with a cozy jungle-style Caribbean experience, making it one of the most iconic lobster restaurants in Cozumel.
